The whole practice

The sequence is a way into the work, not a fixed route. Evidence can change how I understand the problem, and questions may lead me back to earlier thinking. Systemic Thinking runs through every stage in a different way; Fletcher's expertise and judgment encompass the whole.

Explore the practice

Each description remains available here if interactive selection is unavailable.

Investigate

I examine available material and its context. Inventories, source references, repository information, usage, and update indicators can help me decide what deserves closer attention. I may inspect a representative sample in depth, looking at references, links and images, sequence, repository fit, formatting, and procedural completeness.

My judgment matters in deciding what to inspect, how deeply, and what evidence is relevant. Comparison material can provide context without automatically serving as a benchmark. For example, a procedure that appears complete at first may reveal missing prerequisites or implicit steps on closer reading.

Recognize

I distinguish isolated defects from recurring issues and move between artifact-level observations and broader explanations. An interpretation is a hypothesis: evidence can change it, and a plausible explanation still needs to be examined.

My judgment matters in deciding whether observations share an underlying cause and whether that explanation is supported. AI can assist with pattern extraction and problem articulation; it does not settle the conclusion. Several different gaps may point to a common problem, but the pattern needs checking against source material.

Design

I consider whether an established practice addresses the issue, what can be adapted, or whether a different standard is needed. I weigh consequences for human comprehension, AI ingestion and retrieval, and operational reliability.

Requirements address failures that materially compromise function, reliability, or usability; recommendations offer meaningful improvements without making their absence a fundamental failure. My judgment matters in weighing consequences and deciding what is essential.

Question

Iterative questioning can surface decisions not yet formalized, such as ownership, approval conditions, timelines, or lifecycle handling. Reading can expose a missing connection; I identify the question that would bridge it and determine whether I can answer it from my expertise.

My judgment matters in deciding what can responsibly be resolved and what remains unsupported. If a description depends on an unstated approval condition, identifying that missing decision may change what the material needs to explain.

Evaluate

I check whether evidence supports the interpretation, requirements reflect functional consequences, and explanations include necessary dependencies. Comprehension can signal a structural gap. Substantive claims must be ones I believe from my expertise and can defend from my expertise.

My judgment matters in deciding what is coherent and defensible, and what needs more investigation, revision, or removal. Evaluation is not just checking whether an activity is finished.

Communicate

I connect investigation, findings, underlying problems, proposed interventions, and the reasons they matter. Different audiences may need different representations of the same work: authoritative technical material and decision-oriented explanation serve different purposes.

My judgment matters in choosing what an audience needs to understand without losing the connection to evidence. AI can assist with language and simpler representations while professional ownership remains mine.

AI involvement

AI can assist with pattern extraction, problem articulation, questioning, exploring approaches, language refinement, production, simpler representations, and executive-facing presentations. Its contribution varies by activity and its output remains subject to professional review.

Fletcher's role

Accumulated expertise, pattern recognition, interpretation, judgment, intellectual ownership, and integration encompass the practice. This is what makes the activity professionally directed and defensible.

Record-keeping

Recording observations and source information as work progresses, maintaining a continuous daily log, and periodically reviewing or aggregating records makes both specific activity and broader patterns visible.

Systemic Thinking

I move between artifacts and environment, connect observations into patterns, consider how interventions affect other parts of a system, surface dependencies, test coherence, and preserve the relationship between findings, problems, and responses.

Prepare for New Eyes

I anticipate how people unfamiliar with the material will encounter, understand, and use it. This shapes material design and communication to different audiences.

Governance and risk

Risk and governance considerations shape designed responses, questions about ownership or approval, evaluation of functional consequences, and communication of decisions and implications.
